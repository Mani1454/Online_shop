Apna Kirana - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026 | Effective Date: September 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Apna Kirana ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Apna Kirana hyperlocal grocery ordering mobile application and the Shopkeeper Counter Web Portal. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our customers and store partners in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

2. Information We Collect

  • Phone Number: Used solely for secure One-Time Password (OTP) verification and critical delivery communication via SMS or phone call.
  • Delivery Address: Street name, flat/house number, and landmark provided by you to enable neighborhood delivery boys to deliver your orders within 15–25 minutes.
  • Approximate Location: Requested only while selecting your delivery address to calculate store delivery radius and estimate accurate delivery times. We do NOT track background location.
  • Order History: Past items ordered, amounts, and delivery timestamps to facilitate quick 1-tap reordering.

3. Payments & Financial Data

Apna Kirana does NOT store, process, or transmit your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or Netbanking passwords. All online payments are handled directly through National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) verified Unified Payments Interface (UPI) applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) installed on your smartphone, or collected as Cash on Delivery (COD).

4. Data Security & Storage

All communications between your mobile device and our backend servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Data is stored securely in ISO/IEC 27001 certified Google Cloud Platform data centers with automated encrypted backups.

You may request the deletion of your account, phone number, and saved addresses at any time. For privacy inquiries or account removal requests, please contact our store grievance desk:
